No. 01 About

A practical team for complex New York property decisions.

TipTop works across residential, multifamily, rental, commercial, and mixed-use properties. The team helps clients evaluate opportunities, understand affordability and compliance, and move through each step with confidence — without theatrics, and without pretending real estate in this city is simple.

We don't list every house in Queens. We list ours, and we know the streets around them. When a buyer or renter calls about a listing, the person who answers actually walked the block.
